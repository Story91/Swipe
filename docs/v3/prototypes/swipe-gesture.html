Swipe gesture prototype — V3 §7.6

Standalone feel-test for the two-stage swipe (arm, then commit). No build step — this is the approval gate before the gesture is written into the real app. Judge it with a finger, not by reading.

Swipe (or press YES / NO below) to arm a side — its stake chips appear here.

Try this

  • Drag the top card left or right — it tracks your finger 1:1, and the YES/NO side tints as you go.
  • Release past the halfway point, or flick quickly — the card docks to that side instead of flying away, and stake chips appear. Feel for the light haptic on phones.
  • Swipe the same direction again to commit — the card flies off carrying your throw's speed.
  • Swipe the opposite direction while armed to cancel — it springs back to centre.
  • Grab a card mid-flight and reverse it — mid-dock, mid-return, or even mid fly-off. Nothing should ever be locked in; catching a committed card un-commits it.
  • No gesture required: the YES / NO / Cancel / Confirm buttons above do the same thing, with real keyboard focus.
  • Tune the spring feel live in the panel on the right — nothing needs a reload.
